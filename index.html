Where my work ethic comes from

Three years at In-N-Out taught me how to perform under pressure.

I worked my way up to a Level 6 cook, the position that sets the pace and quality for the entire store, earning five promotions over the course of two years. On a busy night that meant turning out upwards of 200 or more burgers in a 30-minute period, often short-staffed, without sacrificing quality. I also trained fellow associates across multiple stations, directly assisting in their promotion(s).

It also developed habits I try to carry everywhere, be it nearly perfect attendance in five years of work (both In-N-Out and my former job), a Hardest Worker Award, and a customer-service mindset of leaving people happier than they were when they came in.
