Where my work ethic comes from

Five years at In-N-Out taught me how to perform under pressure.

I worked my way up to a Level 6 cook, the position that sets the pace and quality for the entire store, earning six promotions over the course of two years. On a busy night that meant turning out upwards of 200 or more burgers in a 30-minute period, often short-staffed, without sacrificing quality. I also trained fellow associates across multiple stations, directly assisting their promotion(s).

It also built habits I carry everywhere: nearly perfect attendance in five years of work, a Hardest Worker Award, and a customer-service mindset of fixing problems fast and leaving people happier than when they came in. That same reliability and steadiness is what I'd bring to a marketing team.
